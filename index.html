Questions General Counsel ask us.

Anything else, ask us in the demo. We answer plainly.

Is our data safe?

Your contracts stay in your repository. legalVector uses the repository’s official APIs, and every call runs as the signed in user, with no super user and no parallel permissions. Visibility is applied on the server, secrets stay on the server and are write only in the interface, and passwords are never stored. The audit trail is mirrored as comments in the repository.

Does it fit the systems we already run?

Yes. It runs on Alfresco Content Services or Doxis 4, each covered by 124 end to end checks. Changes come out as genuine Word tracked changes. Notifications go by email, Microsoft Teams (through a Teams Workflow, no Azure app registration) and Slack, and key dates export to your calendar. There is no data migration and no second repository.

Does the AI approve contracts on its own?

No. The AI reviews against Legal’s playbook and proposes. A lawyer always decides. Approval waits while must fix findings are open, unless a lawyer overrides them with a written reason, recorded on the timeline. Every approved contract comes with its reasons.

Will the business actually use it?

That is the point of the front door. Raising a request from a Word document takes about a minute, the form fills itself from the document, and an email forwarded to the Legal mailbox becomes a prefilled draft. It works in five languages, including Arabic right to left, in light and dark mode, with your brand.

What does it take to get started?

Connect your repository, preview and apply the Legal setup, then pilot with one or two departments before you roll out. Nothing to migrate, and nothing to unwind.
